Reshaping communities, restoring value

We believe clean, well-kept streets aren’t a luxury. Clean My Street connects residents with professional crews, funded by the community, for the community.

Why we exist

Across South Africa, residents live on streets with littered pavements, blocked drains and overgrown verges. Municipal services are stretched, and the gap between what neighbourhoods need and what they receive keeps growing.

Clean My Street started in Johannesburg in January 2024, out of frustration and hope: frustration at the state of our streets, and hope that neighbours, given simple tools, can fix them together. We built a platform that makes it easy to pool money transparently, hire a professional crew and see the result.

Every clean also creates paid work for local crew members, trained in safe roadside work and kitted out in uniform and protective gear.

What we stand for

Transparency

Every rand is accounted for on the project page. Funds are ring-fenced per street and never pooled. Before-and-after photos are published for every clean.

Community first

Clean streets start with residents who care. We give neighbours the tools to take ownership of the space beyond their walls.

Fair funding

If a project doesn’t reach its goal, every contributor is refunded in full. No risk and no lock-in.

Lasting impact

We don’t just pick up litter. Clearing drains and verges helps prevent flooding, keeps pavements walkable and protects property values.
