Getting to know us

We started in January 2024 because too many streets were falling apart. Neighbourhoods were degrading, municipal services weren’t keeping up, and neglected verges and pavements were dragging down the homes and businesses around them.

So we do the work ourselves: grass cutting, weed removal, clearing drains and kerbs, and the regular streetscape maintenance that keeps a road looking cared for. Most of our clients are property owners and business owners who know a clean street is good for everyone on it.

We proudly hire South Africans. Every clean creates paid local work for crews who know these communities, trained in safe roadside work and kitted out in uniform and protective gear.

Our vision

To make the biggest and most significant impact on communities: clean streets and restored neighbourhoods.

Our mission

To deliver clean, well-maintained streets and public spaces, so parks stay clean, walkways stay usable and the whole area becomes a source of community pride.

Our values

Professionalism, reliability and care for the communities we serve. We do the job properly and take accountability for every one we finish.

Why a clean community matters

Safer spaces for everyone

Clearing litter and debris removes tripping hazards. Well-kept public spaces bring neighbours together and discourage vandalism and crime.

Looking after the environment

Less litter and pollution protects people, wildlife and rivers. Clear drains keep stormwater flowing and help prevent flooding.

Restoring property value

Buyers notice neglected streets. A well-maintained road makes an area more attractive and helps protect what homes and businesses are worth.

Continuous care, not just a clean-up

A one-off clean fades. Regular maintenance keeps a street clean and welcoming month after month.

“The more we neglect what lies beyond our walls, the smaller our space becomes.”

Going green

We want to help bring urban spaces back to life, with work that’s good for the community, responsible towards the environment and always getting better.
