Four easy ways to back them

None of these cost you extra, and all of them help.

  1. Shop local first

    Before you buy online or drive across town, check whether a sponsor nearby sells it.

  2. Mention Clean My Street

    Tell them you found them here. It shows them their sponsorship is noticed, and some have a thank-you offer.

  3. Leave a review

    A quick Google review helps a small business more than almost anything else, and it’s free.

  4. Share them

    Post a sponsor’s page in your street or estate WhatsApp group when someone asks for a recommendation.
