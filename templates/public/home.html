From messy verge to clean kerb in four steps

You never pay for a clean that doesn’t happen. Money is held for your street only, and returned if the goal isn’t met.

  1. Request

    Tell us the street and what needs doing. Add a photo if you can. It takes two minutes and no account.

  2. Get a quote

    We assess the job and send a fixed, itemised quote within 48 hours — once-off or monthly upkeep.

  3. Share and fund

    Send the funding link to your street WhatsApp group. Each household chips in their share.

  4. We clean

    Once funded, the crew is booked within 7 days and posts progress photos on your project page.

“The more we neglect what lies beyond our walls, the smaller our space becomes.”

Overgrown verges, blocked drains and sand-covered pavements make streets less safe, flood faster in summer storms and pull property values down. Municipal services are stretched. Clean My Street gives neighbours a simple, transparent way to fix their own street together — and keep it fixed.
