Privacy policy

How Clean My Street (Pty) Ltd collects and uses personal information, in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

What we collect

  • When you request a clean: your name, email, optional phone number, the street details and any photo you upload.
  • When you contribute: your name, email, the amount, and an optional message. Card details are entered on Yoco’s secure checkout; we never see or store them.
  • When you browse: standard server logs (IP address, browser type) kept for security for up to 30 days.

Why we use it

  • To assess your street, send quotes and keep you updated on your request.
  • To process contributions, send receipts and issue refunds.
  • To email contributors about progress on the projects they support.

We don’t sell or rent personal information, and we don’t send marketing emails without your consent.

What is shown publicly

Project pages show contributors’ names (or “Anonymous” if you choose), the amount, and any message you write. Your email address is never shown. Uploaded photos are re-processed to remove location data.

Who we share it with

Yoco (payment processing), our email provider (to send notifications) and our hosting provider, each bound to protect your information. We share information with authorities only where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Payment records are kept for five years as required for tax purposes. Request details are kept for as long as the project is active and for two years afterwards.

Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete your personal information, or object to how we use it, by emailing {{ site.contact_email }}. You may also complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa) at inforegulator.org.za.
