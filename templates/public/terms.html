Terms of use

The rules for requesting cleans and contributing to projects on Clean My Street.

1. The service

Clean My Street (Pty) Ltd publishes funding pages for street-cleaning projects requested by residents, and carries out the work with its own or contracted crews once a project is funded.

2. Contributions

  • Each project has a funding goal, a minimum contribution and a deadline, shown on its page.
  • Contributions are held for that project only and are not pooled with other projects.
  • If the goal is reached by the deadline, contributions are allocated to the project and are not refundable, except where the project is later cancelled by us.
  • If the goal is not reached by the deadline, or the project is cancelled, every contribution is refunded in full to the original payment method.
  • Contributions above the goal are applied to the same street’s project (for example, extra drains or the next maintenance visit) and reported on the project page.

3. Scope of work

We carry out the work described in the published quote. Weather, safety or access issues may change the date; we’ll notify contributors. Work is done on public road reserves only, and does not include private property.

4. Requests

We may decline or ask for changes to any request, for example where the street is outside our service area or the work requires municipal permission.

5. Content

By uploading photos or messages you confirm you have the right to share them and allow us to publish them on the project page.

6. Liability

To the extent permitted by law, our liability for any claim relating to a project is limited to the amount you contributed to it.
